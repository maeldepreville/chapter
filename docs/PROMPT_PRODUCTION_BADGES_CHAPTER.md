# Prompt-cadre — production des badges de Chapter

Ce document est destiné à être remis à un agent spécialisé dans la génération d’images. Il doit être utilisé avec l’**image de référence MM1 déjà approuvée**, qui montre le niveau de matière, de relief, de finition et de stylisation attendu. Cette image sert uniquement d’ancre artistique : sa silhouette et son symbole ne doivent pas être recopiés sur toute la collection.

## Mode d’emploi

1. Joindre l’image MM1 au premier message adressé à l’agent.
2. Copier le prompt principal ci-dessous dans le même message.
3. Faire valider successivement le prototype de famille, chaque lignée, les honneurs, puis la planche complète.
4. Ne demander les exports individuels qu’après la passe d’harmonisation des vingt-et-un badges.

## Prompt principal à transmettre

```text
You are the lead heraldic emblem designer and art director for Chapter, a contemporary editorial reading journal. Your task is to create one coherent family of 21 premium achievement badges: four evolving lineages of four badges each, plus five standalone honorary distinctions.

The attached approved image is the MM1 artistic reference. Study it closely and preserve its material credibility, restrained luxury, relief quality, lighting, and editorial sophistication. Use it as a style anchor only. Do not copy its shield silhouette or its exact book composition across the collection.

CORE ART DIRECTION — NON-NEGOTIABLE

- The badges must look like real manufactured enamel-and-brass medals or insignia from a refined literary order.
- Warm, slightly aged brass; patina limited to recesses; subtly polished edges.
- Deep, opaque enamel fields with believable thickness and reflections.
- Warm ivory enamel for primary symbols and selected interior details.
- Fine brass dividers, precise bevels, and controlled bas-relief.
- Frontal, centered, symmetrical presentation with no perspective distortion.
- Integrated editorial, bookbinding, ex-libris, cartographic, calligraphic, or restrained botanical ornament. Ornament must transform the composition, never accumulate as generic spikes around it.
- Contemporary editorial restraint with an almost chivalric sense of order.
- Every badge must remain legible at small interface sizes.
- No written title, letter, number, numeral, ribbon text, banner text, initials, or typographic inscription inside any badge.
- No people, faces, hands, mascots, crowns, weapons, trophies, generic game-achievement iconography, heroic fantasy, magic glow, science-fiction effects, excessive filigree, or medieval kitsch.

SHARED FAMILY RULES

- Normalize the apparent area, visual weight, border thickness, relief depth, symbol scale, camera, lighting, and framing across all 21 assets.
- The four badges of one lineage keep exactly the same outer silhouette and enamel color.
- A lineage evolves through meaningful changes to its central symbol, internal architecture, relief, and integrated ornament. Never express progression by making the badge larger, brighter, more saturated, or merely adding more spikes, stars, rays, or metal.
- The four invisible design functions are:
  1. Essential sign — the pure symbol and enamel field dominate.
  2. Symbol in action — the symbol begins to act within its field.
  3. Mastered composition — symbol, internal frame, and ornament become one architecture.
  4. Accomplished emblem — the narrative resolves in a complete, balanced composition with one meaningful material or symbolic refinement, not decorative excess.
- Color identifies a family, never a level.
- Generate only the full-color acquired versions. The application will create the locked next-badge state through reversible desaturation and opacity; do not design separate grey assets.

COLOR SYSTEM — WORKING ENAMEL ANCHORS

- Reading: bookbinding brick red, #8A3F36.
- Exploration: muted petrol blue-green, #2E5D5B.
- Expression: deep ink plum, #5D405C.
- Relation: muted moss green, #4F634E.
- Honors: deep midnight blue, #28384F.

Treat these hexadecimal values as production anchors. Preserve their hue identity while adapting only what is necessary for believable enamel, lighting, and contrast. All five families must have comparable visual strength. Brass and ivory remain consistent everywhere.

LINEAGE 1 — READING

Stable outer silhouette: a restrained page-shield, reminiscent of an ex-libris or library plaque, with a subtly arched top and contained lower point. Central symbol: an open book. Never personify or levitate the book.

1. “Adepte des pages” — Essential sign
   A simple frontal open book with a few clearly structured leaves. The silhouette and brick enamel field dominate.

2. “Complice des livres” — Symbol in action
   Both groups of pages unfold and answer one another in a balanced symmetrical rhythm, expressing familiarity with books.

3. “Bibliophile au long cours” — Mastered composition
   Page lines extend into continuous paths that join the inner frame, suggesting a lasting journey through books.

4. “Bibliothèque vivante” — Accomplished emblem
   The open book remains central and becomes part of a complete ex-libris architecture with restrained classical bookbinding and botanical ornament. “Living” means rhythm, continuity, and an inhabited library tradition only: no animation, face, roots, levitation, supernatural light, invasive vegetation, or science-fiction treatment.

LINEAGE 2 — EXPLORATION

Stable outer silhouette: a restrained compass-inspired octagon, without excessive points. Central symbol: a specially drawn compass or compass rose.

1. “Adepte de l’inattendu” — Essential sign
   A clear compass rose whose needle subtly favors a non-cardinal direction.

2. “Esprit nomade” — Symbol in action
   The needle leaves a free, mobile route across the field rather than a straight predetermined path.

3. “Boussole des marges” — Mastered composition
   The route reaches the peripheral contours; map marks and topographic lines structure the octagonal margins.

4. “Horizon vivant” — Accomplished emblem
   Compass, route, contour lines, and several grounded horizon or relief lines form an open but precisely cartographed field. Keep it tangible and cartographic: no dominant constellations, magic light, animated landscape, cosmic imagery, or science-fiction aesthetic.

LINEAGE 3 — EXPRESSION

Stable outer silhouette: a softened vertical lozenge whose controlled elongation recalls a nib. Central symbol: a specially drawn feather or pen nib. The lineage represents both public reviews and curated editorial lists; never represent likes, reactions, ratings, or popularity.

1. “Écho des pages” — Essential sign
   A feather touches one short line, like a first annotation born from reading.

2. “Interprète des œuvres” — Symbol in action
   The feather connects several distinct editorial traces: a critical line, a marginal marker, and a selection mark.

3. “Voix singulière” — Mastered composition
   Lines, margins, and list markers acquire a distinctive cadence and organization, forming an editorial signature without any real letters or readable text.

4. “Conscience des textes” — Accomplished emblem
   Feather, lines, margins, and markers become a balanced heraldic page, expressing a thought conscious of its form and sources.

LINEAGE 4 — RELATION

Stable outer silhouette: a rounded interlaced quatrefoil organized around a center. Central symbol: two specially drawn linked elements. The lineage represents reciprocal literary conversation and the creation of shared space, never follower count or network size.

1. “Présence complice” — Essential sign
   Two distinct links answer one another and share a first point of contact.

2. “Trait d’union” — Symbol in action
   The two links interlace and form a balanced bridge between both sides of the field.

3. “Point de rencontre” — Mastered composition
   The link frames a common center that evokes the literary work around which conversation develops.

4. “Cercle vivant” — Accomplished emblem
   The linked arcs resolve into an open, balanced quatrefoil around the shared literary center. “Living” comes only from rhythm and visual circulation between the arcs: no network nodes, growing graph, people, animation, or vegetation.

FIVE STANDALONE HONORS

The honors do not belong to any axis and do not evolve by level. They all use midnight-blue enamel #28384F, but each has a unique silhouette and unique central emblem. They must feel rarer and fully resolved, not larger, shinier, or more overloaded than the lineage badges.

1. “Première lumière”
   Unique silhouette: a radiant medal with a restrained lower point.
   Emblem: a geometric flame or revealed light emerging from a subtly opened book.
   Meaning: repeatedly discovering confidential works and bringing them into public view.

2. “Atlas partagé”
   Unique silhouette: a refined hexagonal cartouche.
   Emblem: a stylized open atlas crossed by one shared route.
   Meaning: composing an exceptional editorial list across diverse authors, forms, and original languages.

3. “Voix qui relie”
   Unique silhouette: a slightly concave round medal.
   Emblem: two opposing editorial volutes united by one central feather stroke.
   Meaning: a review becoming the origin of several reciprocal literary conversations.

4. “Lien fidèle”
   Unique silhouette: a vertical seal.
   Emblem: a closed double knot around a small editorial clasp.
   Meaning: building a deep recurring literary dialogue with the same person across several works.

5. “Chapitre vivant”
   Unique silhouette: a four-armed cross-rosette.
   Emblem: four page forms organized around one shared center.
   Meaning: reaching advanced mastery across all four lineages.

MANDATORY PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

Do not generate 21 isolated images independently.

Phase A — family proof
- First, analyze the attached MM1 reference and restate the invariants you will preserve.
- Produce one controlled family proof containing five badges at equal scale: one second-stage representative from each lineage and one honor.
- Keep the same camera, light, brass, ivory, relief, enamel behavior, and apparent size.
- Wait for approval before continuing.

Phase B — lineage contact sheets
- Produce one contact sheet per lineage, showing its four stages side by side in semantic order.
- Keep outer silhouette, hue, scale, camera, and lighting strictly constant within the sheet.
- Make each evolution recognizable through structure at thumbnail size.
- Revise the entire four-badge sheet together when one member breaks family coherence.

Phase C — honor contact sheet
- Produce all five honors together at equal apparent scale on one sheet.
- Verify that their silhouettes and symbols are clearly distinct while their midnight-blue enamel, brass, ivory, relief, and lighting remain identical.

Phase D — global harmonization
- Assemble all 21 approved badges on one final audit sheet.
- Normalize scale, border weight, brass temperature, ivory value, enamel depth, patina, relief, shadow softness, ornament density, and symbol legibility.
- Correct any badge that appears more prestigious merely because it is larger, brighter, sharper, more saturated, or more decorated.

Phase E — final exports
- Export each badge separately as a centered square PNG with transparent background, minimum 2048 × 2048 px.
- Preserve comfortable transparent padding and identical apparent object size.
- Also export the complete 21-badge audit sheet on a warm neutral paper background.
- Use stable ASCII filenames:
  reading-01-adepte-des-pages.png
  reading-02-complice-des-livres.png
  reading-03-bibliophile-au-long-cours.png
  reading-04-bibliotheque-vivante.png
  exploration-01-adepte-de-l-inattendu.png
  exploration-02-esprit-nomade.png
  exploration-03-boussole-des-marges.png
  exploration-04-horizon-vivant.png
  expression-01-echo-des-pages.png
  expression-02-interprete-des-oeuvres.png
  expression-03-voix-singuliere.png
  expression-04-conscience-des-textes.png
  relation-01-presence-complice.png
  relation-02-trait-d-union.png
  relation-03-point-de-rencontre.png
  relation-04-cercle-vivant.png
  honor-01-premiere-lumiere.png
  honor-02-atlas-partage.png
  honor-03-voix-qui-relie.png
  honor-04-lien-fidele.png
  honor-05-chapitre-vivant.png

Before every approval request, explicitly audit:
- family coherence;
- silhouette correctness;
- semantic evolution;
- color-family correctness;
- small-size legibility;
- absence of text and forbidden imagery;
- equal apparent prestige;
- fidelity to the MM1 material and lighting reference.
```

## Résultat attendu

L’agent ne doit pas chercher à finaliser immédiatement vingt-et-un fichiers. Il agit comme directeur artistique d’une collection : il verrouille d’abord la famille, construit chaque série dans son ensemble, puis corrige les écarts sur une planche globale avant l’export.

Les futurs états verrouillés ou « prochain badge » ne nécessitent aucun asset supplémentaire. L’interface appliquera une désaturation et une opacité réversibles au fichier couleur afin de préserver une source unique par badge.
